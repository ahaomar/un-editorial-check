# Claim → evidence audit

One row per claim this tool makes in its user-facing documents: whether it is supported, what bounds it, and the test that locks it. Evidence names the suite that runs in `npm test`; CI runs the same chain plus explicit source-registry validation. A claim is never stated as supported without a row.

## What no row claims

No row asserts completeness of detection, accuracy or truth of copy, neutrality of outcome, legal conformity, or institutional endorsement. Those are outside a local rule run by construction: the tool screens copy against documented, enabled local rules and reports what those rules find.
