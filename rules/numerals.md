# Numerals and claims

- **UE-NU001** — Dates in running prose use day–month–year, for example `9 November 2026`. Dates in source code, data formats, URLs and immutable references are excluded from future semantic expansion; the v0.2.0 pattern remains intentionally conservative.
- **UE-NU002** — Numeric ranges use an en dash: `1990–2025`.
- **UE-RE003** — Prose figures need a hedge such as “approximately”, “at least” or “an estimated”, and should identify source and date.
- **UE-DI001** — A coverage count must state what is counted: member economies, economies with a value, or economies drawable on a map. Human review is required for semantic accuracy.

The checker does not calculate ratios, validate years against API rows, or prove that prose matches rendered data. These are Tier 2 review tasks.
